# Governance

1. Joshua Shane and J. Scott King release their work together as co-founders of Signal 37 (S37).
2. The Verification Kit is Joshua Shane's. He holds its copyright, and decides what changes and when it is released.
3. The checks are generated from a single master and are not hand-edited in this repository. A change is made in the master and the tree is rebuilt. The `.generated` file records when.
4. Releases are recorded in the Releases section of `LICENSING.md`, which is append-only. No versioned release has been cut yet.
5. Contributions arrive as issues (`CONTRIBUTING.md`). The author adopts what he takes up, with credit.
